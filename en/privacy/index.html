Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

What you type

Every tool does its calculations in your browser. The values you enter are never sent to a server, and Toolburrow doesn’t store them.

What this site doesn’t do

  • No Google Analytics or any other analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No accounts. We never ask for your name, email or other personal details.
  • No ads at the moment.

Data stored in your browser

If you use the light/dark switch at the top of the page, the site remembers your choice in your browser’s localStorage (a single value, “light” or “dark”). It stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is removed when you clear the site’s data. The site sets no cookies.

Third-party services

To load the site, your browser connects to the services below. They receive your IP address and other standard connection details, and handle them under their own policies:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the heading font. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Affiliate links

Some pages may later include clearly marked affiliate links to products. If you follow one, you go to the merchant’s site, which may use a cookie to record that you came from Toolburrow so it can pay a commission. That happens on the merchant’s site under its own privacy policy. Toolburrow only sees the click and sales totals the merchant reports, never your name or contact details.

Ads in the future

The site may add Google AdSense ads later. This policy will be updated before that happens. Google and its partners may then use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Google explains how it uses cookies in advertising, and you can turn off personalized ads in your Google ad settings.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date above. For questions, see Contact.

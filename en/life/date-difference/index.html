How the days between two dates are counted

The number of days is the end date minus the start date, so the start date itself isn’t counted. From October 1 to October 10 is 9 days.

If you want the total number of days, for example for a trip, a hospital stay or time off, both the first and last day count, so add 1: that’s 10 days. Tick “Include both the start and end date” to do this automatically.

How months and days are counted

The calculator first counts whole calendar months from the start date, then the days left over. From March 15 to October 19 is 7 months to October 15, plus 4 days: 7 months, 4 days.

When the next month has no matching day, the last day of that month is used. So January 31 plus 1 month is February 28, or February 29 in a leap year. Leap years are handled automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Does “days until” include today?

Usually not, and the calculator doesn’t by default. If today is October 19, October 20 is 1 day away.

Which date is “within 30 days”?

It depends on whether the start date counts. Counting from the day after, 30 days from October 19 ends on November 18. Counting October 19 as day 1, it ends on November 17. Use the “Count the start date as day 1” option to switch. Contracts, bills and laws can define deadlines differently, so check the exact wording.

Are weekends and holidays excluded?

No. The calculator counts calendar days, including weekends and public holidays. For working days, subtract the weekends and the holidays where you live.

Do leap years change the result?

Yes, and the calculator handles them. February has 29 days in a leap year, so a period that crosses February in a leap year is one day longer. The next leap year is 2028.
